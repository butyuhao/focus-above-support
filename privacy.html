隐私政策 简体中文

Focus Above · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 4 日

1. 本机数据

Focus Above 在设备上保存你填写的旅客姓名、偏好、专注与航程记录，以及恢复进行中旅程所需的进度，用于生成机票、统计和恢复专注。App 不要求注册独立的 Focus Above 账户，也不请求实时位置。

2. 可选的 iCloud 同步

拥有会员权限并开启 iCloud 同步后，偏好、航程记录和删除标记会发送至 Apple 账户下的 iCloud，用于设备间同步。同步受账户、网络和同步容量影响。关闭开关会停止后续同步，不会删除已有云端数据。Focus Above 不为这些记录运营独立服务器。

3. 购买和地图

App 内付款由 Apple 处理。Focus Above 读取商品信息和已验证的交易权益以解锁功能，不接收银行卡详情。地图使用 Apple 地图服务，可能通过网络请求地图内容，其数据处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。航班时刻表为内置参考数据，并非实时航班信息。

4. 诊断与支持

Focus Above 不包含广告或第三方广告追踪。Apple 可能根据你的系统与测试设置提供崩溃报告和 TestFlight 反馈。联系支持时，我们使用你主动提供的邮箱和内容来回复及排查问题。请勿发送密码或银行卡信息。

5. 保留与管理

你可以修改旅客姓名和偏好、关闭 iCloud 同步，并在 App 中删除航程。删除标记用于防止记录经同步再次出现。为防止数据损坏，本机恢复副本可能保留先前记录。卸载 App 会移除本机数据，但不会移除已有 iCloud 数据。如需处理恢复副本、云端数据或曾向我们发送的信息，请联系支持。我们无法直接访问你的 Apple 账户。

6. 联系与更新

如有隐私问题或相关请求，请通过下方邮箱联系 Focus Above 支持。数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会在 App 中更新本政策及日期。本政策随 App 提供，无网络也可阅读。

focusabove.support@gmail.com

Privacy Policy English

Focus Above · Updated October 4, 2026

1. Data on your device

Focus Above stores your chosen traveler name, preferences, focus and journey records, and progress needed to resume a journey on your device. These support tickets, statistics and session recovery. The app does not require a separate Focus Above account or request your live location.

2. Optional iCloud sync

If you have membership access and enable iCloud sync, your preferences, journey records and deletion markers are sent to Apple’s iCloud under your Apple Account for use across devices. Sync depends on your account, connectivity and available sync capacity. Turning sync off stops further syncing; it does not remove existing cloud data. Focus Above does not operate a separate server for these records.

3. Purchases and maps

Apple processes in-app payments. Focus Above reads product information and verified transaction entitlements to unlock features; it does not receive your payment card details. Maps use Apple’s map services and may request map content over the network under Apple’s privacy policy. Flight schedules are bundled reference data, not live flight information.

4. Diagnostics and support

Focus Above has no advertising or third-party advertising trackers. Apple may provide crash reports and TestFlight feedback according to your system and testing choices. If you contact support, we use the email address and information you choose to send to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment card information.

5. Retention and your choices

You can edit your traveler name and preferences, turn off iCloud sync, and delete journeys in the app. Deletion markers help keep deleted journeys from reappearing through sync. Local recovery copies may retain earlier records to protect against corruption. Removing the app removes its local data, but not existing iCloud data. Contact support for help with recovery, cloud data or requests concerning information you sent us. We cannot directly access your Apple Account.

6. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact Focus Above support at the address below. When data handling changes, we will update this policy and its date in the app. This policy is bundled with the app and can be read offline.

focusabove.support@gmail.com
